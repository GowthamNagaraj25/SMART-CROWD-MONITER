About The Project

Smart Tourist Crowd Monitoring System

This application is designed to help tourists plan their trips more effectively by providing real-time insights into crowd levels at popular destinations. By offering data on the best times to visit and suggesting less crowded alternatives, we aim to enhance the tourist experience and reduce over-tourism in specific hotspots.

Key Features

  • Search for specific tourist places by name.
  • View detailed information including location, crowd level, and best time to visit.
  • Discover nearby alternative places that are less crowded.
  • Color-coded indicators for quick assessment of crowd levels.

Technology Stack

This full-stack application uses:

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: Node.js, Express.js
  • Database: PostgreSQL
